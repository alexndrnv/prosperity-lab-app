Prosperity Lab Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 22, 2026  |  Last updated: June 24, 2026

Summary: Prosperity Lab is a free and ad-free financial-literacy learning game. Most app information remains on the user’s device. Artwork is transmitted outside the device only when the user voluntarily submits it to the free community artwork gallery or deliberately shares or exports it.

1. Developer and contact

Prosperity Lab is developed and published by the independent developer of Prosperity Lab.

Privacy questions and artwork-removal requests may be sent to alexndrnv@gmail.com.

2. About Prosperity Lab

Prosperity Lab is a free, ad-free financial-literacy education game. The current release does not require users to create an online account and does not use third-party advertising or developer analytics.

The app includes a local art canvas and a free community artwork gallery. The artwork gallery does not support buying, selling, auctions, commissions, paid listings, seller payments or other commercial transactions between users.

Prosperity Lab is not offered or directed to users in the European Union, European Economic Area or other territories excluded from the app’s Google Play distribution settings.

3. Information stored locally on the device

Prosperity Lab may store the following information locally on the user’s device:

  • Learning progress and completed activities
  • App settings and accessibility preferences
  • Study-reminder preferences
  • Simulated financial-game progress and decisions
  • Artwork created or saved through the art canvas
  • Local technical, security or integrity status information

This locally stored information is used to provide app functionality. It is not automatically transmitted to the developer.

4. Art canvas

Users may create and save artwork through the Prosperity Lab art canvas. Artwork created in the art canvas remains on the user’s device unless the user deliberately chooses to submit it to the community artwork gallery, export it or share it through another application or service.

The app does not require access to the device camera and does not request broad access to the user’s photo, video or media library for the art canvas.

5. Free community artwork gallery

Users may voluntarily submit artwork created through the Prosperity Lab art canvas for review and possible display in the app’s free community artwork gallery.

When a user submits artwork, Prosperity Lab may transmit and process:

  • The submitted artwork image
  • Any artwork title, description or other information deliberately entered in the artwork-submission form
  • Limited technical information needed to receive, review, store, protect and display the submission

Artwork submission is optional. Artwork that is approved and posted in the community gallery may be visible to other Prosperity Lab users. Users should not include personal, confidential, financial or sensitive information in submitted artwork, titles or descriptions.

Submitted artwork may be reviewed, rejected, hidden or removed when it is unlawful, abusive, inappropriate, misleading, infringing, unsafe or inconsistent with the community artwork rules.

Posting artwork does not transfer ownership of the artwork to Prosperity Lab. By submitting artwork, the user gives Prosperity Lab permission to receive, review, store, reproduce and display the submitted artwork only as reasonably necessary to operate and protect the free community artwork gallery.

The community artwork gallery is not a sales marketplace. Artwork cannot be bought or sold through Prosperity Lab, and users do not receive payments, commissions, prizes, rewards or other compensation for submitting artwork.

6. Information Prosperity Lab does not collect

The current release does not collect the following information for the developer:

  • Precise or approximate location
  • Device contacts or address-book information
  • Telephone-call information
  • SMS or text-message information
  • Microphone recordings
  • Camera recordings
  • Government identification numbers
  • Bank-account details
  • Payment-card details
  • Advertising identifiers for targeted advertising

Prosperity Lab does not sell users’ personal information or submitted artwork.

7. Sharing, exporting and referrals

When a user chooses to share Prosperity Lab, share an app link, or export or share artwork, the app may open the device’s standard share sheet, browser, email application or another external application selected by the user.

The user chooses the content, external application, message and recipient. Prosperity Lab does not upload the user’s contacts and does not verify whether an external message, referral or shared file was delivered or opened.

Information deliberately provided to an external application or service is processed according to that external service’s own privacy policy and terms.

8. Local reminders and notifications

Prosperity Lab may allow users to schedule optional study reminders. These reminders are scheduled locally on the device.

Users may control reminder frequency, timing, sound and whether device notifications or in-app reminders are enabled. Android may ask the user for permission before displaying notifications.

The current release does not use remote marketing notifications and does not send advertising notifications.

9. Voluntary creator support

Prosperity Lab may provide an optional link that allows users to make a voluntary donation to support the developer or the project.

Donations are optional and do not provide digital goods, artwork, subscriptions, premium features, marketplace privileges, recognition, rewards, improved game results or additional app functionality.

When the user opens the support page, Prosperity Lab opens a Stripe-hosted HTTPS payment page in the device’s system browser. Stripe, rather than Prosperity Lab, processes the payment credentials.

Prosperity Lab does not receive or store the user’s complete payment-card details. Information submitted to Stripe is processed according to Stripe’s own privacy policy and terms.

10. Service providers

Prosperity Lab may use limited service providers to host public webpages, operate the community artwork gallery, protect submitted content or process voluntary donations.

These providers may process only the information reasonably necessary to perform their services. Their handling of information is also governed by their own privacy policies, security practices and legal obligations.

11. Permissions

Depending on the device and Android version, Prosperity Lab may use:

  • Internet access for user-initiated HTTPS links
  • Internet access for voluntary artwork submissions
  • Notification access for optional local study reminders
  • Vibration for reminder alerts
  • Audio-settings control for ordinary in-app sound playback
  • Device-restart functionality when necessary to restore reminders previously scheduled by the user

The current Android release does not request broad location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, telephone, storage or media-library, package inventory, package installation or screen-overlay permissions.

12. Retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains on the device until the user deletes it, resets the app or uninstalls the app.

Users may erase locally stored Prosperity Lab progress and settings through Settings → Reset all progress. Uninstalling the app also removes app-local information. Android cloud backup is disabled for the current release.

Artwork submitted to the community artwork gallery may remain stored separately from the app’s local data. Resetting or uninstalling the app may therefore not automatically remove artwork that was previously submitted.

A user may request removal of submitted artwork by emailing alexndrnv@gmail.com and providing enough information to identify the artwork. Removal requests will be reviewed and handled within a reasonable period, except where temporary retention is reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, dispute resolution or compliance with legal obligations.

13. Children

Prosperity Lab is intended for teenagers age 13 and older and for adults. It is not directed to children under 13.

Users should not submit artwork containing personal information about a child or identifying information about another person without appropriate permission.

14. Security

Prosperity Lab uses reasonable technical measures intended to protect app information and submitted artwork. Configured external links are restricted to HTTPS, cleartext network traffic is disabled, and payment credentials or secret payment keys are not embedded in the app.

No electronic storage, transmission or software-security method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

15. International processing

Service providers used for webpage hosting, artwork hosting or voluntary donation processing may operate in countries other than the user’s country. Information voluntarily submitted to those services may therefore be processed in other jurisdictions, subject to the service provider’s safeguards and applicable law.

16. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Prosperity Lab’s features, data practices, service providers or legal obligations change.

The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when the policy changes. Material changes should also be reflected in the app and in the applicable Google Play Data Safety declarations before the changed release is distributed.
