Overview

Prosperity Lab is a free, ad-free financial-literacy education app. The current release does not create an online account and does not use advertising or developer analytics.

Information stored on the device

The app stores learning progress, settings, reminder preferences, simulated financial-game information, locally created art and local security/integrity events on the user’s device. This information is used to provide app functionality and is not transmitted to the developer by default.

Information the app does not collect

The current release does not collect names, postal addresses, precise location, contacts, telephone data, SMS data, photographs, videos, microphone recordings, card details, bank details, government identifiers or advertising identifiers for the developer.

Sharing and referrals

When a user chooses to share Prosperity Lab, the app opens the device’s standard share sheet. The user chooses the external application, message and recipient. Prosperity Lab does not upload the user’s contacts and does not verify delivery or installation. The selected external service processes information under its own privacy policy.

Local reminders

Optional study reminders are scheduled locally on the device. Users control reminder frequency, timing, sound and whether device or in-app reminders are enabled. The app does not use a remote marketing-notification service in this release.

Voluntary creator support

If the user opens the optional support page, the app opens a verified Stripe or PayPal HTTPS page in the system browser. The payment provider, not Prosperity Lab, processes payment credentials. Prosperity Lab does not receive card details and does not receive or store payment-completion status. The provider’s own privacy policy and terms apply.

Permissions

The Android release uses internet access for user-initiated HTTPS links, audio-settings control for local playback and vibration for local reminders. Broad location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, telephone, storage/media, package inventory, package installation and overlay permissions are not requested by the app.

Retention and deletion

Local app information remains on the device until the user deletes it. Users can erase Prosperity Lab’s stored state through Settings → Reset all progress. Uninstalling the app also removes app-local data; Android cloud backup is disabled for this release.

Children

Prosperity Lab is intended for teenagers age 13 and older and adults. It is not directed to children under 13.

Security

The app restricts configured external links to HTTPS, disables cleartext network traffic and does not embed payment credentials or secret payment keys. No method of software security is absolute.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app’s data practices or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised. Material changes should be reflected in the app and Google Play Data Safety declaration before the changed release is distributed.
