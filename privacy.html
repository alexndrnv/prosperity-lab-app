Prosperity Lab Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 22, 2026  |  Last updated: June 24, 2026

Summary: Prosperity Lab is a free and ad-free financial-literacy learning game. The current release does not require an account, does not use advertising or developer analytics, and stores normal app progress and settings locally on the user’s device.

1. Developer and privacy contact

Prosperity Lab is developed and published by the independent developer of Prosperity Lab.

Privacy questions and requests may be sent to alexndrnv@gmail.com.

2. About Prosperity Lab

Prosperity Lab is a free, ad-free financial-literacy education game intended for adults age 18 and older.

The current release does not require users to create an online account and does not use third-party advertising or developer analytics.

The current Google Play release is not offered or directed to users in the European Union or European Economic Area.

3. Information stored locally on the device

Depending on the features used, Prosperity Lab may store the following information locally on the user’s device:

  • Learning progress and completed activities
  • App settings and accessibility preferences
  • Study-reminder preferences
  • Simulated financial-game progress and decisions
  • Local technical, security or integrity status information

This information is used to provide app functionality. The current release does not automatically transmit this locally stored information to the developer.

4. Information the app does not collect

The current Prosperity Lab release does not collect the following information for the developer:

  • Names through an app account or registration process
  • Postal addresses
  • Precise or approximate physical location
  • Device contacts or address-book information
  • Telephone-call information
  • SMS or text-message information
  • Photographs or videos
  • Camera recordings
  • Microphone or voice recordings
  • User-created content
  • Government identification numbers
  • Bank-account credentials
  • Complete payment-card details
  • Advertising identifiers for targeted advertising

Prosperity Lab does not sell user information.

5. Sharing the app and external links

When a user chooses to share Prosperity Lab or an app referral link, the app may open the device’s standard share sheet or another external application selected by the user.

The user chooses the external application, message and recipient. Prosperity Lab does not upload the user’s contacts and does not verify whether an external message or referral was delivered, opened or acted upon.

Prosperity Lab does not provide chat, direct messaging, user profiles, image sharing, audio sharing, file sharing or other user-to-user content exchange in the current release.

The app may open user-selected HTTPS webpages in the device’s external browser. Information processed after the user opens an external service is subject to that service’s own privacy policy and terms.

6. Local reminders and notifications

Prosperity Lab may allow users to schedule optional study reminders. These reminders are scheduled locally on the device.

Users may control reminder frequency, timing, sound and whether device notifications or in-app reminders are enabled. Android may ask the user for permission before displaying notifications.

The current release does not use remote marketing notifications and does not send advertising notifications.

7. Voluntary support and Stripe

Prosperity Lab may provide an optional link through which users can make a voluntary support payment or tip.

Voluntary support does not provide digital goods, subscriptions, premium features, recognition, rewards, rankings, access, improved game results or additional app functionality.

Selecting the support link opens a Stripe-hosted HTTPS payment page in the device’s external browser. Stripe, rather than the Prosperity Lab app, processes payment credentials.

The Prosperity Lab app does not receive or store complete payment-card numbers, card security codes or online banking passwords.

Stripe may provide the Prosperity Lab Developer with limited transaction information needed for payment support, recordkeeping and dispute handling. Depending on the information entered by the user, this may include the payment amount, date, transaction status, receipt or transaction identifier and contact information.

Information entered on the Stripe-hosted page is processed according to Stripe’s own privacy policy, terms and data-retention practices.

8. Support communications

When a user voluntarily contacts the support email address, the Prosperity Lab Developer may receive the user’s email address, message and any information or attachments the user chooses to provide.

Support communications are used to answer questions, investigate technical issues, respond to privacy requests and review mistaken-payment inquiries.

Users should not send passwords, complete card numbers, card security codes, government identification documents or other unnecessary sensitive information by email.

9. Public website and external service providers

The Prosperity Lab public information and privacy-policy webpages may be hosted through an external web-hosting provider. The hosting provider may process standard technical web-request information, such as an IP address, browser information and request time, under its own privacy practices.

Stripe is used only when a user voluntarily opens the external support page. Prosperity Lab does not use Stripe for in-app purchases, subscriptions or access to app functionality.

10. Android permissions and device access

Depending on the device and Android version, Prosperity Lab may use:

  • Internet access for user-initiated HTTPS links
  • Notification access for optional local study reminders
  • Vibration for reminder alerts
  • Audio-settings control for ordinary local sound playback
  • Device-restart functionality when needed to restore reminders previously scheduled by the user

The current Android release does not request broad location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, telephone, storage or media-library, package inventory, package installation or screen-overlay permissions.

11. Retention and deletion

Information stored locally remains on the device until it is removed through available app controls, the app is reset or the app is uninstalled.

Users may erase locally stored Prosperity Lab progress and settings through Settings → Reset all progress. Uninstalling the app also removes app-local information. Android cloud backup is disabled for the current release.

Support emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request, maintain support records, prevent abuse or meet applicable legal obligations.

Stripe controls the retention of information entered on its hosted payment page. The Prosperity Lab Developer may retain limited transaction records where reasonably necessary for support, accounting, dispute handling or legal compliance.

A user may request deletion of support communications or other information held directly by the Prosperity Lab Developer by emailing alexndrnv@gmail.com. Some limited information may be retained when reasonably required for legal, accounting, security or dispute-resolution purposes.

12. Adult audience

Prosperity Lab is intended for adults age 18 and older. It is not designed or directed to children or minors.

13. Security

Prosperity Lab uses reasonable technical measures intended to protect app information. Configured external links use HTTPS, cleartext network traffic is disabled, and complete payment credentials or secret payment keys are not embedded in the app.

No method of electronic storage, transmission or software security can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Prosperity Lab’s features, data practices, service providers, distribution or legal obligations change.

The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when the policy changes. Material changes should also be reflected in the app and in the applicable Google Play Data Safety declarations before the changed release is distributed.

15. Contact

Prosperity Lab Developer
Email: alexndrnv@gmail.com
