Prosperity Lab

Prosperity Lab is a free, ad-free financial-literacy learning game featuring lessons, stories, simulations and practical decision-making activities for adults.

About the app

Prosperity Lab is designed to help adults explore personal finance, budgeting, business, economics, project management, investment concepts and simulated financial markets in an educational environment.

Prosperity Lab does not open financial accounts, execute real investments, lend money, transfer funds, act as a broker or provide individualized regulated financial, legal, tax or investment advice. Financial and market activities within the app are educational simulations.

Adult audience

Prosperity Lab is intended for adults age 18 and older. It is not designed or directed to children or minors.

Availability

The current Google Play release is not offered or directed to users in the European Union or European Economic Area. Availability is controlled through the countries and regions selected for distribution in Google Play.
