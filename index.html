Prosperity Lab

Prosperity Lab is a free, ad-free financial-literacy educational mobile app featuring lessons, stories, simulations, and creative learning tools.

About the app

Prosperity Lab is designed to help users explore personal finance, business, economics, and simulated markets in an educational environment. The app does not open financial accounts, execute real investments, lend money, transfer funds, or provide individualized regulated financial advice.
